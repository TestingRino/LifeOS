Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Telos is a planning app. Your plan (goals, tasks, journal, calendar items) is personal, so the rule we build by is simple: your plan belongs to you, it stays where you put it, and we do not sell it or use it for ads. This page explains exactly what is stored, where, and how to delete it.

Signing in, and what we ask Google for

You can sign in with Google, Apple or an email address and password, or use Telos with no account. Signing in with Google or Apple gives us your email address and name as that provider shares them, and nothing else. We do not receive your password for Google or Apple, and an email password is stored only as a salted hash by Supabase.

Google permissions Telos can ask for:

  • Your email address and basic profile (openid, email, profile). To create your account and show which account is signed in.
  • Google Drive “app data” (drive.appdata), older mode only. To keep your plan and backups in a private folder in your own Drive. Telos cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar, read only (calendar.readonly). Optional. To show your events next to your plan. Asked only if you connect your calendar.
  • Google Calendar, creating events (calendar.events). Optional and asked separately, only when you use a feature that adds events to your calendar (photo imports, scheduled tasks, your ideal week). If you never use those, Telos never asks for it.

Telos's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to provide the features you see in the app. We do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features, do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it unless you ask for support and share it.

Photo import

If you import a photo of a planner or calendar, the image is sent through the Telos server to an AI service (Anthropic) to read the text. The server does not store the photo or the result. The AI provider handles the image under its own terms and privacy policy. You review everything before it is added to your plan. Photo import is currently limited to approved accounts.

Plans and payments

If you subscribe to a paid plan, payment is handled by Stripe on its own hosted pages. We never see or store your card number. We keep your plan, its status and renewal date, and a Stripe customer ID so we can show your plan and let you manage billing. The Telos operator can see accounts, plans and photo-import usage counts (not your plan contents) in an admin screen, to run the service and give support you ask for.

Photo import waitlist

If you ask for early access to photo import, we collect your email address and the planner you use. We use them only to tune photo reading for that planner and to email you about access. They are stored with our hosting and database providers (see below), and we do not use them for marketing or share them. To be removed, open an issue at the contact link below or reply to any access email, and we will delete your entry.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Supabase hosts accounts and the stored copy of your plan.
  • Google and Apple (sign-in, and Calendar if you connect it) under their own terms.
  • Vercel hosts the small Telos server that passes calendar requests through, relays photos, and receives anonymous events. Like any host, it keeps routine request logs (time, address, route).
  • Stripe (only if you subscribe) processes payments.
  • PostHog (if enabled) receives the anonymous events described under Analytics.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Anthropic reads photos you choose to import.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Analytics

To learn whether the app is useful, Telos records a few anonymous milestones, such as “opened the landing page”, “finished setup” or “completed a first weekly review”. Each event carries only its name, the app version, and an identifier (a random install ID, or your account ID once you sign in). It never includes the contents of your plan, and your email address is not sent unless the operator has explicitly turned that on and said so here. Telos honors your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals, and you can switch analytics off in Settings at any time.

Cookies and storage

Telos does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your plan and sign-in on your device, and a service worker so the app can open offline.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Export everything any time from Settings → Your data → Export backup.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Your data → “Delete everything, everywhere”. This erases your plan on the device, permanently deletes your account and the copy in our database (and any older Google Drive copy), and revokes Telos's access to your Google account.
  • Revoke access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and remove any leftover app data under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.
  • Without an account, your plan exists only in your browser. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes it.

Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. The app restricts what it can load and who it can talk to, and sign-in tokens never travel in web addresses. No online service is perfectly secure, so keep a backup of anything you cannot lose.

Children

Telos is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and note it in the app. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/TestingRino/LifeOS/issues (please do not include personal information in a public issue).

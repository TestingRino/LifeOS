Life.OS

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Life.OS is a planning app. Your plan (goals, tasks, journal, calendar items) is personal, so the rule we build by is simple: your plan belongs to you, it stays where you put it, and we do not sell it or use it for ads. This page explains exactly what is stored, where, and how to delete it.

Where your information lives

WhatWhereWho can see it

Anonymous usage events (see “Analytics” below)Our hosting logs and, optionally, an analytics serviceThe Life.OS operator

The Life.OS server does not keep a database of users or plans. It never stores your plan.

What we ask Google for, and why

  • Your email address and basic sign-in (openid, email). To show which account is signed in and to identify you for approved features.
  • Google Drive “app data” (drive.appdata). To keep your plan and backups in a private folder in your own Drive, so you can use another device and recover from a lost phone. Life.OS cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar, read only (calendar.readonly). Optional. To show your events next to your plan. Asked only if you connect your calendar.
  • Google Calendar, creating events (calendar.events). Optional and asked separately, only when you use a feature that adds events to your calendar (photo imports, scheduled tasks, your ideal week). If you never use those, Life.OS never asks for it.

Life.OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google data only to provide the features you see in the app. We do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide those features, do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it unless you ask for support and share it.

Photo import

If you import a photo of a planner or calendar, the image is sent through the Life.OS server to an AI service (Anthropic) to read the text. The server does not store the photo or the result. The AI provider handles the image under its own terms and privacy policy. You review everything before it is added to your plan. Photo import is currently limited to approved accounts.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Google (sign-in, Drive, Calendar) under your Google account's terms.
  • Vercel hosts the small Life.OS server that exchanges Google sign-in codes, passes calendar requests through, and relays photos. Like any host, it keeps routine request logs (time, address, route).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website itself.
  • Anthropic reads photos you choose to import.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Analytics

To learn whether the app is useful, Life.OS records a few anonymous milestones, such as “opened the landing page”, “finished setup” or “completed a first weekly review”. Each event carries only its name, the app version, and a random install ID that is not linked to your name, email or Google account. It never includes the contents of your plan. Life.OS honors your browser's Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals, and you can switch analytics off in Settings at any time.

Cookies and storage

Life.OS does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your plan and sign-in on your device, and a service worker so the app can open offline.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Export everything any time from Settings → Your data → Export backup.
  • Delete everything from Settings → Your data → “Delete everything, everywhere”. This erases your plan on the device, deletes the copy and backups in your Google Drive, and revokes Life.OS's access to your Google account.
  • Revoke access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and remove any leftover app data under Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.
  • Without an account, your plan exists only in your browser. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes it.

Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. The app restricts what it can load and who it can talk to, and sign-in tokens never travel in web addresses. No online service is perfectly secure, so keep a backup of anything you cannot lose.

Children

Life.OS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and note it in the app. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/TestingRino/LifeOS/issues (please do not include personal information in a public issue).

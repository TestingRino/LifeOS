# Telos — User Guide for Friends & Family

Welcome! This guide explains how to use Telos.

## Getting Started (30 seconds)

1. **Open this link:** https://testingrino.github.io/LifeOS/
2. **Start using it** — an account is optional. Sign in with Google (Apple or email when enabled) to keep your plan safe and use it on any device
3. **Everything stays private** — your data never leaves your browser

That's it. You're good to go.

---

## What Can You Do?

### 📋 Plan Your Life

- **Foundation:** Write your vision, what you want people to say at your funeral, your values
- **Goals:** Set 10-12 goals per planning cycle tied to your roles (Parent, Professional, etc.)
- **Month:** Plan priority actions for each month in 6 life areas
- **Week:** Plan your week, track habits, log workouts, rate how you did
- **Today:** See your day's tasks, upcoming rituals, goal progress

### 📊 Track & Measure

- Weight log with trend chart
- Workout history (12-week view, by type)
- Habit tracker (heatmap showing consistency)
- Goal progress with sparkline trends

### 📸 Upload Your Planner (Optional)

If your friend set up Calendar sync:

1. Click **Settings** ⚙
2. Click **Upload photo**
3. Take a photo of your planner (schedule, goals, habits, etc.)
4. Choose where to add it ("Add to schedule", "Create Calendar event", etc.)
5. Done — data auto-fills 🎉

Supported photos:
- 📅 Calendar/schedule pages
- ⭐ Goals
- 🏃 Workout logs
- ✅ Habit trackers
- 🛒 Shopping lists
- 📓 Journal notes
- 🎯 Bucket list
- And more!

### 📸 Photo import: which planners work

Choose **Planner page** and (optionally) your planner brand: Full Focus, Panda, Passion, Day Designer, Franklin Covey, Bullet Journal, Happy Planner, Erin Condren, Moleskine / Leuchtturm, Hobonichi, Cultivate What Matters, Simplified / Ink+Volt / Clever Fox, or another. Telos reads priorities (your Big 3), the hourly schedule, to-dos with their checkboxes, notes and gratitude, and habit-tracker ticks. You can choose several photos at once, such as every day of a week. Bullet-journal symbols (•, ×, >, <, ○, –, *) are understood too.

Everything goes through a review screen first: fix anything misread and untick what you don't want. Reading handwriting is never perfect, so check the dates and times in particular.

### 📅 Google Calendar Sync (Optional)

1. Settings ⚙
2. Click **Connect Google Calendar**
3. Log in to Google
4. Grant permission
5. Your calendar shows up in your Telos Week view ✅

## Tasks

**Tasks** (top menu) is one list for everything you need to do. Each task can have a due date, a day to do it, a time, a priority, a project, tags, subtasks, notes, a goal it serves and a status: **Next**, **Waiting** (for someone), **Someday** or **Done**. Tasks show up on Today, on the Week page, under the goal they belong to, and on the calendar when they have a time.

- **Add quickly.** Type naturally: `Call dentist friday !high #health +Admin`. Dates (today, tomorrow, friday, in 2 weeks, 3/14), `!high`/`!med`/`!low`, `#tags`, `+Project`, `@waiting` and `~someday` are understood. Press **n** anywhere.
- **Repeat.** `Pay rent monthly on the 1st`, `Take out trash every monday`, `every 2 weeks`. When you finish one, the next appears. Open a task to choose weekdays, "every N", or "count from when I finish it". Skip one with ⤼.
- **Plan by dragging.** Drag a task onto a day in the Week page to plan it for that day, or onto a time slot on the calendar to time-block it.
- **Move undone to tomorrow.** On Today (and the Tasks page) one tap moves everything you did not finish: tasks, planned priorities and your Big 3. Repeating tasks and habits stay put, because they are not meant to pile up.
- **Templates.** *From template* adds a ready-made checklist: weekly review, monthly money review, trip checklist (counted back from your departure date), project plan, hosting a gathering and more. Save any project as your own template.
- **Reminders.** Set a reminder in a task, and turn on notifications in Settings. Reminders and a morning digest appear while Telos is open, or installed on your phone or computer and running in the background. A fully closed app cannot be woken without a push service.
- **Works offline.** After the first visit Telos opens without a connection and syncs when you are back online.

## Search, undo and the Archive

- **Search** (press `/`, or Ctrl/⌘ K, or tap 🔍): searches tasks, goals, milestones, habits, notes, journal, memories, lists, key dates, your purpose, vision and legacy. Type `task:` or `goal:` first to narrow.
- **Undo** (Ctrl/⌘ Z, or the Undo button after a delete or move) steps back through your recent changes. Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z redoes.
- **Archive** holds items you deleted for 60 days (restore any), finished tasks older than 30 days, and finished or dropped goals.

## What I finished (review summaries)

Under **Plan**. Pick week, month, quarter or year to see tasks, milestones, priorities, Big 3, habits, workouts and where your effort went across life areas. **Copy**, **Download .md** or **Print / PDF**.

## Printing for a paper planner

**Print** on Today, Week and Month opens a clean page for your printer or "Save as PDF" (choose Landscape and turn off headers and footers).

## Life areas

Goals, the balance view and the Wheel of Life share ten areas: Career, Finances, Family, Faith, Fitness, Mental & emotional, Learning, Community & service, Personal & social, and Home. The Foundation page also has a **legacy** prompt.

## Tips & Tricks

**Copy to your paper planner:**
- Month page → click "Copy to your monthly spread"
- Week page → click "Copy to your weekly spread"
- Paste into your physical planner

**Recurring habits:**
- Add a habit once, it carries forward each week
- Check off your wins in the habit tracker
- Watch your consistency in the Track view

**Weekly review ritual:**
- Every Friday (or day before your week ends): visit the Week page
- Rate your week (1-5)
- Update goal pulse (on track / slipping / stalled)
- Review what went well, what to improve
- This feeds your reflections and insights

**Goal linking:**
- When you add monthly or weekly actions, link them to goals
- This shows you how your daily tasks connect to your bigger vision
- The app alerts you if a goal has no actions planned

## Privacy & Data

**Your data is yours:**
- Your plan is stored in your browser, and in your account if you sign in
- Nothing sent to servers (except optional Calendar + photo OCR)
- No ads, no tracking, no data selling
- You control export/import

**Photos:**
- Sent to Claude AI for OCR processing
- Processed, then deleted immediately
- Not stored anywhere

## Troubleshooting

**"I lost my data"**
- Did you export a backup? Import it: Settings → Import backup
- If not, it's lost (sorry). Export regularly going forward.

**"Calendar sync not working"**
- Try reconnecting: Settings → Disconnect Calendar → Connect again
- Make sure you have internet connection

**"Photo OCR failed"**
- Try a clearer photo (good lighting, focused, not blurry)
- Make sure the photo clearly shows the data (schedule, goals, etc.)

**"I want to use on my phone"**
- Open Telos in Safari or Chrome
- Tap Share → Add to Home Screen
- Now it's an app on your phone! 📱
